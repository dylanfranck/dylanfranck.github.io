Another tool built for an audience of one. It has three views:

- **Savings buckets:** goals with targets and progress.
- **Card strategy:** which card to use for what, and the rewards trade-offs.
- **Investments:** allocation and growth, charted.

It saves by writing a new copy of itself with the data embedded, which is convenient and
exactly why it can't be published. Like the PTO planner, it's waiting on a private,
signed-in home.
