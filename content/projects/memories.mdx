Social media is built for the moment: post it now, while it's happening. I wanted the
opposite, a place to collect the photos and notes from a day and come back to them later,
when there's room to think about what they meant.

The current prototype is a memory timeline that loads my own data and lays it out
chronologically. Open questions I'm still working through:

- **Why not just a diary, or a private Instagram?** Pulling from the places photos already
  live (with real API connections) and having time for contemplation are the bets.
- **Privacy and storage:** it's the most personal data I have, so it can't be a public
  page. It needs real authentication, and arguably encryption I control.
- **Inheritance:** a long-horizon idea that a lifetime of memories could become something
  passed down, like a photo album that keeps growing.

## Personal tools behind auth

This, the [PTO planner](/projects/pto-calculator/), and the
[finance dashboard](/projects/finance-dashboard/) share a problem: they're only useful
with my real data, so they need to be reachable from anywhere and locked to me. That's
the next piece of infrastructure I want to build for this site's spokes.
