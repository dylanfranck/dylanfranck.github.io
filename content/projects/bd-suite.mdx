An internal product, so this page stays high level.

**Direction:** upload an RFP and get a transparent, factor-by-factor win-probability
estimate (no opaque LLM score), then track the opportunity through the pipeline. A
retrieval-augmented assistant searches past performance and prior bids to help draft responses.

**Stack:** a Python modular monolith (Streamlit today, with a React + FastAPI migration
under way) over PostgreSQL, with semantic matching and RAG for the AI pieces.
